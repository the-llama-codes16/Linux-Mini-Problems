Once upon a time, there were three little pigs. One pig built a house of straw, the second pig built a house of sticks, and the third pig built a house of bricks. The first two pigs built their houses quickly and then sang and danced all day because they were happy.

But the third pig worked hard all day and built his house with strong bricks. A big bad wolf saw the first little pig in his straw house. He said, Little pig, little pig, let me come in! But the pig answered, Not by the hair on my chinny chin chin.

So the wolf huffed and puffed and blew the house down. The little pig ran to his brother's house made of sticks. The wolf followed and said, Little pig, little pig, let me come in! But the pig said, Not by the hair on my chinny chin chin.

Again, the wolf huffed and puffed and blew the house down. The two pigs ran to their brother’s brick house. The wolf came and said, Little pig, little pig, let me come in! But the third pig said, Not by the hair on my chinny chin chin.

Then the wolf huffed and puffed, but he could not blow the brick house down. He climbed to the roof and tried to get in through the chimney. But the clever pig had a pot of boiling water waiting in the fireplace. The wolf fell in and that was the end of him.

The three little pigs lived happily ever after.
